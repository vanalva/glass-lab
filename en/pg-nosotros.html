01 // About us

Pernia Glass

Architectural glass systems made in Panama to an international standard. We combine the precision of Italian design with the efficiency of local production to deliver high-end solutions at competitive prices.

02 // Mission

Our Mission

To open up access to high-end architectural glass systems across the region. We believe every project deserves glass solutions that combine aesthetics, function and durability, without trading quality for price.

Experience

With more than a decade of experience in glass processing, our founder Guillermo Pernia has developed a manufacturing process that matches the quality of European systems -- high-precision anodised aluminium profiles, tracks with stainless steel bearings and certified tempered glass -- with competitive lead times and local technical support.

03 // Backing

Backed by Glass Lab

Glass Lab is our experimental glass laboratory -- where new compositions, textures and finishes are researched. That work feeds directly into Pernia Glass systems, giving our clients access to decorative and functional glass found nowhere else in the Central American market.

04 // Quality

Certifications and Warranty

ICON

Quality Control

Visual and dimensional inspection on every unit produced. ASTM C1048 strength testing.

ICON

Certified Materials

6063-T5 aluminium with class AA25 anodising. EN 12150 tempered and EN 14449 laminated glass.

ICON

Structural Warranty

10-year warranty on profiles and hardware. 5 years on tempered glass. Ongoing technical support.

ICON

Production Capacity

Glass up to 3,100 x 2,150mm. In-house tempering furnace and lamination. Delivery in 15-20 business days.
