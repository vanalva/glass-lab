01 — What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device (computer, tablet or mobile phone) when you visit them. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to site owners.

Cookies can be "persistent" or "session" cookies. Persistent cookies stay on your device for a set period or until you delete them manually. Session cookies are removed automatically when you close your browser.

03 — How to manage cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways. Most browsers let you see which cookies you have stored and delete them individually, or block cookies from specific sites or from all sites.

Please note that if you delete or block cookies, some site features may be unavailable or may not work properly. To manage cookies, see your browser's help section or visit the cookie settings on our site.
